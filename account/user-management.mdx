PathPilot makes it straightforward to manage who has access to your compliance workspace. From a single settings page, you can invite new team members, assign them the right role, organize them into functional teams, and remove access when needed. Keeping your user list accurate and up to date is an important part of maintaining a strong access control posture — especially when preparing for a SOC 2 audit.

## Inviting Users

Adding a new team member to PathPilot takes just a few clicks. When you send an invitation, PathPilot emails the recipient a secure link to accept and set up their account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open User Settings">
    Go to **Settings → Users** in the left navigation panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite User">
    Click the **Invite User** button in the top-right corner of the Users page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter details and assign a role">
    Enter the person's email address and select the appropriate role from the dropdown — Admin, Editor, Auditor, or Viewer. For a full breakdown of what each role can do, see the [Permissions](/account/permissions) guide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Send Invite**. PathPilot immediately sends an invitation email to the address you entered. If your organization has SSO configured, the invitee will be directed to sign in through your identity provider. Otherwise, they'll be prompted to set a password and activate their account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Pending Invites

After sending an invitation, it appears in the **Pending Invites** section of the Users page until the recipient accepts it. From there, you have full control over outstanding invitations.

To **resend** an invite — for example, if the email expired or was missed — click the three-dot menu next to the pending invite and select **Resend Invitation**. A fresh invitation email will be sent immediately.

To **revoke** an invite before it's accepted, click the three-dot menu and select **Revoke Invitation**. The invitation link is invalidated instantly, and the intended recipient will no longer be able to use it to join your workspace.

## Removing Users

When a team member leaves your organization or no longer needs access to PathPilot, you should remove them promptly to maintain least-privilege access.

To remove a user, go to **Settings → Users**, find the person in the list, click the **three-dot menu** next to their name, and select **Remove**. Their access is revoked immediately — active sessions are terminated and they will not be able to log in again.

Removing a user does not delete their historical activity. All audit log entries, evidence uploads, and workflow actions they performed are retained in full, ensuring your compliance records remain complete and accurate for audits.

## Teams

Teams let you group users by function — such as Engineering, Legal, Security, or HR — and use those groups as a unit when assigning controls, tasks, and workflows. Instead of assigning work item by item to individuals, you can assign an entire control or task to a team, making ownership clear at a glance and simplifying handoffs when people change roles.

Teams are managed from **Settings → Teams**. Each user can belong to multiple teams, and teams can be updated at any time without affecting historical assignments.

## Creating a Team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    Go to **Settings → Teams**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new team">
    Click **New Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your team">
    Enter a descriptive name for the team — for example, `Security`, `Legal`, or `Engineering`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members">
    Search for and add users from your workspace to the team. You can add as many members as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The team is now available to assign to controls, evidence tasks, and workflows throughout PathPilot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## SCIM Provisioning

For organizations that need to manage users at scale, PathPilot supports automated user provisioning and deprovisioning through the **SCIM 2.0** protocol. With SCIM enabled, your identity provider automatically creates PathPilot accounts when employees are onboarded and deactivates them when they leave — eliminating the need for manual invite and removal workflows.

SCIM provisioning is available on the **Enterprise plan** and can be configured with Okta, Azure Active Directory, or any SCIM 2.0-compatible IdP. To set it up, go to **Settings → SSO → SCIM Provisioning** and follow the setup instructions for your IdP. You'll be provided with a SCIM base URL and bearer token to enter in your identity provider's configuration.

Once active, user creation, updates (such as name or role changes synced from your directory), and deactivation are all handled automatically, keeping your PathPilot access list perfectly in sync with your HR or IT systems.

<Note>
  Only users with the **Admin** role can invite new users, remove existing users, manage teams, and configure SCIM provisioning. If you don't see the Invite User button or Settings → Teams, contact your PathPilot Admin to adjust your role.
</Note>
