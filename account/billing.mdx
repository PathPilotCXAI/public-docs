PathPilot offers flexible plans designed to meet teams at every stage of their SOC 2 compliance journey — from startups running their first audit to large enterprises with complex compliance programs. This guide explains what's included in each plan, how billing works, and how to manage every aspect of your subscription from within the app.

## Plans

PathPilot has three pricing tiers. All plans include access to the core PathPilot platform; the tiers differ in team size, automation capabilities, and enterprise-grade features.

**Starter** is built for small teams and organizations beginning their SOC 2 journey. It supports up to **5 users** and includes access to PathPilot's core SOC 2 control library, manual evidence collection and management, and basic compliance reporting. Starter is ideal for early-stage companies that want to establish a solid compliance foundation before scaling their program.

**Pro** is designed for growing teams that are ready to automate and accelerate their compliance work. It supports up to **25 users** and adds automated evidence collection workflows, third-party integrations (such as AWS, GitHub, and Jira), advanced reporting and audit-ready exports, and SSO for centralized identity management. Pro is the right choice for teams actively preparing for or maintaining a SOC 2 Type II certification.

**Enterprise** is PathPilot's most comprehensive offering, built for large organizations with sophisticated compliance requirements. It includes **unlimited users**, SCIM-based automated provisioning, custom role creation, a dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM), a service-level agreement (SLA), and support for custom contracts and procurement processes. Enterprise customers can also work with PathPilot to configure the platform to align with additional frameworks beyond SOC 2.

## Billing Cycle

PathPilot subscriptions are billed on either a **monthly** or **annual** cycle, in **USD**. You choose your preferred billing frequency when you first subscribe, and you can switch between cycles when managing your subscription.

Organizations that commit to an annual plan receive a **20% discount** compared to the equivalent monthly rate — a meaningful saving for teams with a long-term compliance program. Annual plans are billed as a single upfront charge at the start of each subscription year.

## Managing Your Subscription

You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your PathPilot subscription at any time directly from the app — no need to contact support for standard plan changes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing Settings">
    Go to **Settings → Billing** from the left navigation panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Manage Subscription">
    Click the **Manage Subscription** button. You'll be taken to the subscription management portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Select **Upgrade**, **Downgrade**, or **Cancel** depending on your needs. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm your selection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Updating Payment Method

To update the credit or debit card on file for your PathPilot subscription, go to **Settings → Billing → Payment Method** and click **Update Card**. Enter your new card details and save. PathPilot accepts the following payment methods:

- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express

Invoices and payment receipts are automatically sent to the **billing contact email** configured on your account. To update the billing email, go to **Settings → Billing** and edit the billing contact details.

## Invoices

PathPilot generates an invoice automatically at the start of each billing cycle. You can download past invoices at any time for your financial records, expense reporting, or audit documentation.

To access your invoice history, go to **Settings → Billing → Invoice History**. Each invoice is available as a downloadable PDF and includes a full breakdown of the charges for that period, your plan details, and tax information where applicable.

## Cancellation

You can cancel your PathPilot subscription at any time without penalty. To cancel, follow the steps in [Managing Your Subscription](#managing-your-subscription) above and select **Cancel**.

After cancellation, your account remains fully active and accessible until the **end of your current billing period** — you won't lose access the moment you cancel. Once the billing period ends, your workspace enters a read-only state. Your data is retained for **30 days** after cancellation, giving you time to export any controls, evidence, or reports you need. After 30 days, all workspace data is permanently deleted in accordance with PathPilot's data retention policy.

If you decide to reactivate your account before the 30-day window closes, contact [sales@pathpilot.com](mailto:sales@pathpilot.com) and the team can restore your workspace.

<Warning>
  Downgrading your plan may immediately remove access to features tied to your current tier. For example, downgrading from **Pro** to **Starter** will disable automated workflows, third-party integrations, and SSO. Users beyond the Starter seat limit (5 users) will lose access. Review what's included in the destination plan carefully before confirming a downgrade, and export any data you may need from features that will no longer be accessible.
</Warning>

The PathPilot sales team can also help you model the cost difference between monthly and annual billing for your specific team size.
