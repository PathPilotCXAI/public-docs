## Response Times

The table below summarizes the support channels and SLAs available on each PathPilot plan:

| Plan | Channels | Response SLA |
|------|----------|--------------|
| **Starter** | Email | 1 business day |
| **Pro** | Email + Live Chat | 4 hours |
| **Enterprise** | Email + Live Chat + Phone | 1 hour |

Response SLAs apply during PathPilot's standard support hours (Monday–Friday, 9 am–6 pm ET) unless otherwise specified in your Enterprise agreement. Enterprise customers with a dedicated Customer Success Manager can also contact their CSM directly for urgent escalations outside of standard hours.

## Enterprise Support

Enterprise customers receive a premium support experience designed for organizations where compliance is business-critical.

When you join PathPilot on an Enterprise plan, you're assigned a **dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM)** who serves as your primary point of contact. Your CSM understands your frameworks, your audit timeline, and your team's workflow — so you're never starting from scratch when you reach out.

Enterprise support includes:

- **Dedicated Customer Success Manager** — a named contact who knows your compliance program and proactively flags risks and opportunities
- **Quarterly business reviews** — structured check-ins to review your compliance posture, upcoming audit milestones, and product roadmap items relevant to your organization
- **Onboarding assistance** — hands-on help configuring integrations, mapping controls to your environment, and training your team during the first 30 days
- **Named technical contacts** — up to two named technical contacts who can escalate issues directly to PathPilot engineering when needed

To learn more about Enterprise plans or to upgrade, email [sales@pathpilot.com](mailto:sales@pathpilot.com).

## Community & Resources

Before opening a support ticket, you may find your answer faster in one of these resources:

- **Documentation** — you're already here. Use the search bar at the top of this page or browse the sidebar to find guides, API references, and integration walkthroughs.
- **Changelog** — see what's new, what's changed, and what's been fixed at [pathpilot.com/changelog](https://pathpilot.com/changelog). We publish release notes with every deployment.
- **Status page** — check real-time platform health and subscribe to incident notifications at [status.pathpilot.com](https://status.pathpilot.com). If there's an active incident, it will be posted there before our team can respond to individual tickets.

<Tip>
  When contacting support, always include your **workspace ID** (found in **Settings → General → Workspace ID**) and a clear description of the steps to reproduce your issue. This allows our team to locate your account immediately and skip the back-and-forth — getting you to a resolution faster.
</Tip>
