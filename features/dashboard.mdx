The PathPilot dashboard is your compliance command center. Every time you log in, you get an immediate, unified view of where your SOC2 program stands — what's passing, what needs attention, and what's coming up on the horizon. Rather than piecing together status from spreadsheets and email threads, the dashboard surfaces the information that matters most so you and your team can act quickly and stay audit-ready at all times.

## Compliance Score

Your Compliance Score is the headline metric at the top of the dashboard. It reflects the percentage of controls that are currently in a **Passing** state across all active frameworks. The score is color-coded so you can assess your posture at a glance:

- 🟢 **Green (80–100%)** — Your program is in strong shape. Most controls have evidence in place and no critical gaps.
- 🟡 **Yellow (50–79%)** — There are notable gaps that need attention before your next audit window.
- 🔴 **Red (0–49%)** — Significant remediation work is required. Prioritize failing controls immediately.

The score updates in real time as evidence is collected, controls are reviewed, and workflow runs complete. Hovering over the score ring shows a breakdown by framework, so you can see whether a dip is isolated to a specific standard or widespread across your program.

## Control Status Summary

Below the Compliance Score, the **Control Status Summary** gives you a count of every control in your program organized by its current state:

- **Not Started** — Controls that have no evidence attached and no owner assigned.
- **In Progress** — Controls where work is underway but evidence collection or review is incomplete.
- **Passing** — Controls that meet all requirements and have up-to-date, accepted evidence.
- **Failing** — Controls with missing evidence, expired artifacts, or a reviewer rejection.

Each status badge is a quick link — click **Failing**, for example, to jump directly to a filtered list of controls that need remediation. This makes it easy to triage issues without navigating through menus.

## Open Tasks

The **Open Tasks** widget surfaces every task assigned to you or members of your team, along with their due dates and associated controls. Tasks are sorted by urgency, with overdue items highlighted in red. You can:

- Click any task to open its detail view and take action directly.
- Reassign a task to another team member without leaving the dashboard.
- Mark a task complete once the underlying work is done, which automatically updates the linked control's status.

If you manage a team, toggle the **My Tasks / All Tasks** switch to see the full picture across every owner in your organization.

## Upcoming Milestones

The **Upcoming Milestones** panel keeps your most important deadlines visible so nothing sneaks up on you. Milestones include:

- **Audit dates** — Your scheduled SOC2 audit window start and end dates.
- **Evidence review deadlines** — Dates by which evidence for specific controls must be reviewed and accepted.
- **Control review cycles** — Recurring review periods (e.g., quarterly access reviews) that require sign-off from a control owner.

Milestones are pulled from your audit calendar and workflow schedules automatically. You can click any milestone to see which controls or tasks are tied to it, giving you a clear action plan for what needs to happen before that date arrives.

## Recent Activity

The **Recent Activity** feed gives you a running log of everything that has changed in your compliance program. It captures events such as:

- Evidence files uploaded or rejected
- Controls updated or reassigned
- Workflow runs completed or failed
- New team members added or roles changed
- Integrations connected or re-authorized

Each activity entry shows the timestamp, the user who performed the action, and the specific resource affected. You can click any entry to navigate directly to the relevant control, task, or workflow run for more context. The feed is scoped to your organization by default, but you can filter it to show only your own activity.

## Customizing Your View

PathPilot lets you tailor the dashboard to match your role and focus area. Use the toolbar at the top of the dashboard to apply filters and rearrange widgets:

- **Filter by Framework** — Narrow the Compliance Score, Control Status Summary, and Open Tasks to a specific framework such as SOC2 Type II or ISO 27001.
- **Filter by Owner** — Scope the dashboard to controls and tasks assigned to a specific team member. This is especially useful for managers reviewing their team's workload.
- **Filter by Status** — Focus only on Failing or In Progress items to prioritize remediation work.
- **Pin Widgets** — Drag widgets to reorder them, or click the pin icon on any widget to lock it in place. Your layout preferences are saved per user, so your dashboard looks the same every time you log in.

To reset to the default layout at any time, click **Restore Defaults** in the dashboard toolbar.

<Tip>
Before an upcoming audit, filter your dashboard by **Failing** and **In Progress** controls and share the filtered URL with your compliance lead. This gives everyone on the team a single, focused view of exactly what needs to be resolved before the auditors arrive — no status meetings required.
</Tip>
