PathPilot is a compliance and security workflow platform built to help engineering, security, and operations teams achieve SOC2 certification — and stay compliant long after the audit ends. Instead of wrestling with spreadsheets and chasing down teammates for screenshots, PathPilot centralizes your controls, automates evidence collection, and gives you a real-time view of your compliance posture at every stage of the journey.

Whether you're preparing for your first SOC2 Type I audit or maintaining continuous readiness for Type II, PathPilot gives your team the structure, automation, and audit trail you need to move fast without cutting corners.

## How PathPilot Works

Getting from zero to audit-ready follows a clear, repeatable path. Here's the high-level onboarding flow:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Create your PathPilot account at [app.pathpilot.com](https://app.pathpilot.com). You can sign up with your work email or via Google or GitHub SSO. Once your account is created, you'll land in the onboarding wizard that guides you through your first workspace setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Your Workspace">
    A workspace is an isolated environment that holds all of your organization's controls, evidence, workflows, and audit logs. Give your workspace a name that reflects your organization or team, then select SOC2 as your compliance framework to pre-populate a curated library of controls mapped to the Trust Service Criteria.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Controls">
    Browse the SOC2 control library and activate the controls that apply to your environment. Assign an owner to each control, set a target status, and link existing policy documents or evidence artifacts. PathPilot tracks the status of every control in real time so you always know where you stand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Your Team">
    Compliance is a team sport. Invite your colleagues and assign them roles — **Admin**, **Auditor**, or **Viewer** — so each person has the right level of access. Admins can configure the workspace and manage integrations, Auditors can review and comment on evidence, and Viewers get read-only visibility into your compliance posture.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  SOC2 readiness is an ongoing process, not a one-time event. PathPilot is designed to support continuous compliance — your controls, evidence, and audit logs remain active and up to date between audits, so your next review is never starting from scratch.
</Note>
