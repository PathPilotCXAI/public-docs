---
title: "PathPilot Quickstart: Set Up Compliance Tracking in Minutes"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"
description: "Create your PathPilot account, configure your first workspace, activate SOC2 controls, and connect your first integration in under 10 minutes."
---

PathPilot is designed to get you from signup to compliance tracking as quickly as possible. This guide walks you through every step — from creating your account to connecting your first integration — so you can start building audit-ready controls right away. Most teams complete this setup in under 10 minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Head to [app.pathpilot.com](https://app.pathpilot.com) and sign up using your work email, or authenticate instantly with Google or GitHub SSO.

    After signing up with email, PathPilot sends a verification link to your inbox. Click the link to confirm your address and activate your account. Once verified, you'll be directed into the onboarding wizard to set up your first workspace.

    <Info>
      Use your work email address rather than a personal one. PathPilot uses your email domain to help teammates find your organization when they sign up.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a Workspace">
    A workspace is your organization's private environment inside PathPilot — it holds all your controls, evidence, workflows, members, and audit logs.

    When prompted by the onboarding wizard:

    1. **Name your workspace** — use your organization's name or team name (for example, `Acme Corp` or `Acme - Security Team`).
    2. **Select a compliance framework** — choose **SOC2** to pre-load a curated library of controls aligned to the SOC2 Trust Service Criteria (Security, Availability, Confidentiality, Processing Integrity, and Privacy).
    3. **Choose your SOC2 categories** — select the Trust Service Criteria categories that apply to your product. At minimum, the **Security** category is required for all SOC2 audits.

    PathPilot immediately populates your workspace with a set of ready-to-activate controls based on your selections.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite Your Team">
    Compliance work is distributed across engineering, security, HR, and legal. Bring your teammates into PathPilot so everyone can own the controls relevant to their domain.

    To invite teammates, navigate to **Settings → Members → Invite** and enter their email addresses. Assign each person one of the following roles:

    - **Admin** — full access to configure the workspace, manage integrations, adjust settings, and invite or remove members.
    - **Editor** — can activate and update controls, upload and manage evidence, and configure workflows, but cannot manage members or change workspace settings.
    - **Auditor** — can view all controls and evidence, leave comments, and flag issues, but cannot modify controls, evidence, or workspace settings. Ideal for external auditors or internal compliance reviewers.
    - **Viewer** — read-only access to the compliance dashboard and control statuses. Ideal for executives or external stakeholders who need visibility without edit access.

    Invited teammates receive an email with a direct link to join your workspace.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Your First Control">
    Controls are the individual security and compliance requirements you need to satisfy for your SOC2 audit. PathPilot provides a pre-built control library so you don't have to start from scratch.

    To activate your first control:

    1. Navigate to **Controls** in the left sidebar and browse the library.
    2. Select a control — for example, **CC6.1: Logical and Physical Access Controls** — and click **Activate**.
    3. **Assign an owner** from your team members. The owner is responsible for keeping the control up to date and providing evidence.
    4. **Link evidence** — attach an existing policy document, screenshot, or test result that demonstrates the control is implemented. You can upload files directly or link to an external URL.

    The control's status updates automatically as evidence is added and reviewed, moving through **Not Started → In Progress → Passing**.

    <Warning>
      Every activated control needs an assigned owner. Controls without owners are flagged as at-risk in your compliance dashboard and may raise questions during an audit.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect an Integration">
    Manual evidence collection is time-consuming and error-prone. PathPilot integrates with the tools your team already uses to collect evidence automatically on a schedule or in response to real-time events.

    To connect your first integration, navigate to **Integrations** in the sidebar and choose from:

    - **Slack** — receive notifications when controls fall out of compliance or when evidence is due for renewal.
    - **GitHub** — automatically pull code review records, branch protection settings, and deployment logs as evidence for development-related controls.
    - **AWS** — connect your AWS account to collect configuration snapshots, IAM policies, CloudTrail logs, and security group rules as automated evidence.

    Follow the OAuth or API key prompts for your chosen integration. Once connected, PathPilot begins collecting evidence on the schedule you define and links artifacts to the relevant controls in your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  After completing setup, visit your **Dashboard** for a bird's-eye view of your compliance posture. The dashboard shows your overall readiness percentage, controls by status (Passing, In Progress, Failing, Not Started), upcoming evidence renewal dates, and any open remediation tasks — so you always know exactly where to focus next.
</Tip>
