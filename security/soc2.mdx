## What Is SOC2?

SOC2 (System and Organization Controls 2) is an auditing standard developed by the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA). It defines criteria for managing customer data based on five **Trust Service Criteria (TSC)**: Security, Availability, Confidentiality, Processing Integrity, and Privacy.

**Type I vs. Type II** is a distinction worth understanding:

- **SOC2 Type I** is a point-in-time audit. An auditor reviews whether your controls are *designed* appropriately as of a specific date. It is a useful milestone for early-stage companies but provides limited assurance about whether those controls actually work day-to-day.
- **SOC2 Type II** is an audit over a sustained period — typically 6 to 12 months. An auditor tests whether your controls *operated effectively* throughout the entire observation window. This is the gold standard that enterprise buyers and security-conscious customers require.

The five Trust Service Criteria give auditors a structured framework for evaluating different aspects of a service organization's controls:

| Criterion | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Security** | Protection of information and systems against unauthorized access |
| **Availability** | System availability for operation and use as committed |
| **Confidentiality** | Protection of confidential information throughout its lifecycle |
| **Processing Integrity** | Complete, valid, accurate, timely, and authorized processing |
| **Privacy** | Personal information collected, used, retained, and disclosed appropriately |

Security is the only mandatory criterion. Organizations choose additional criteria based on the nature of their services and what their customers need assurance over.

## How PathPilot Helps You Achieve SOC2

PathPilot is not just a compliant platform — it is the tool you use to *build* your own SOC2 program. Here is how the platform accelerates your certification:

**Control mapping.** PathPilot ships with a pre-built control library that maps directly to the AICPA's Trust Service Criteria. Instead of building your control framework from scratch, you start with a comprehensive baseline and customize it to match your environment. Every control is tagged to the TSC criteria it satisfies, so you always know exactly where you stand against the standard.

**Evidence collection.** Gathering evidence is the most time-consuming part of any SOC2 audit. PathPilot automates evidence collection by integrating directly with your cloud providers, identity systems, HR tools, and ticketing systems. When an auditor asks for 12 months of access reviews, you pull them from PathPilot in seconds rather than hunting through email threads.

**Status tracking.** PathPilot gives every control a real-time status — passing, failing, not started, or in remediation — so you always have an accurate picture of your compliance posture. Assign controls to owners, set due dates, and track remediation tasks all in one place.

**Auditor-ready reports.** When your audit begins, PathPilot generates structured evidence packages that map directly to auditor request lists (PBCs). Instead of exporting spreadsheets and zipping screenshots, you share a secure link to an organized, timestamped evidence repository that auditors can navigate on their own.
