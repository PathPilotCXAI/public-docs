PathPilot Quality is a quality analytics product for customer-facing teams. It monitors, scores, and coaches every conversation your agents (human or AI) have, using configurable rubrics tailored to your business.

## What it does

- Ingests conversations from Zendesk, Intercom, and file-based sources.
- Scores every interaction with a rubric that fits your product, tone, and compliance needs.
- Surfaces real-time quality metrics, trends, and agent performance in a dashboard.
- Routes conversations to the correct QA flow (for example, COPC-style scoring for support, default scoring for other contexts).
- Supports multi-agent conversations by segmenting the transcript per agent before scoring.
- Captures structured feedback (with Linear integration) so quality findings drive improvements.

## Who it is for

- Support and collections leaders who want 100% QA coverage instead of a random sample.
- Ops teams running multiple contact centers, BPOs, or AI agents that need consistent scoring.
- Compliance and training teams that need auditable scoring evidence and coaching notes.

## How scoring works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Conversations are harvested">
    Tickets and messages are pulled from your integrated source (Zendesk, Intercom, or file) into a normalized schema.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A rubric is selected">
    The right rubric is chosen based on context: COPC for support-heavy queues, default for others, or a custom rubric you configured.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Every conversation is scored">
    Each subcriterion is evaluated with LLM analysis. Scores roll up to categories, then to a final conversation score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Results are surfaced">
    Metrics and detailed scoring show up in the dashboard for reviewers and managers. Feedback can be pushed into Linear.
  </Step>
</Steps>
