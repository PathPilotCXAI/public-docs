PathPilot Loan Origination is a WhatsApp-first agent that walks applicants through a complete loan application. It collects the data and documents your product requires, validates them, calls your decisioning engines, and communicates the outcome, all inside the applicant's chat.

## What it does

- Guides applicants through a customer-configurable application flow.
- Collects required data and documents through native WhatsApp components, buttons, list messages, and Flows.
- Validates fields and documents for completeness, readability, expiration, applicant match, and consistency.
- Integrates with your pre-approval and approval engines and normalizes outcomes into a standard set of decisions.
- Communicates status, missing requirements, and final outcomes back to the applicant in plain language.
- Escalates to a human when policy, compliance, or applicant frustration requires it.

## Who it is for

- Consumer and SMB lenders originating loans through WhatsApp instead of web forms.
- Fintechs that already have decisioning engines and want a conversational front door.
- Teams in Latin America and other WhatsApp-first markets where chat is the primary channel.

## Applicant experience

<Steps>
  <Step title="Applicant starts or resumes on WhatsApp">
    The agent identifies the customer, product, locale, and applicable application flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agent asks only for what is missing">
    Data is collected through native WhatsApp components. Progress is saved so applicants can resume later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Documents are collected and validated">
    Uploads are checked automatically. Mismatches trigger corrections in plain language, without exposing internal rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decisioning runs">
    Once minimum data is available, pre-approval runs. On completion, the final approval engine is called and the response is normalized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Outcome is communicated">
    The applicant receives an approval, conditional approval, decline, or next-step message, or is handed to a human when needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Multi-tenant by design

Each customer connects their own WhatsApp Business number and account. PathPilot handles onboarding, webhook routing, credentials, agent orchestration, and validation, but the applicant-facing sender identity stays as your customer's approved number.
